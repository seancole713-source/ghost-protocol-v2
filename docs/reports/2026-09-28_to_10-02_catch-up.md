## How to read the numbers

- **Morning card:** the premarket list. It is due by 8:28 CT (9:28 ET).
  Each stock gets a verdict:
  - **ELIGIBLE** means the rule would take it.
  - **REJECTED** means it would not.
  - **DATA_UNAVAILABLE** means there was no fresh price to judge it.
- **Radar:** the intraday scanner. It watches movers after the open and can
  make its own trade setups.
- **Size:** each setup buys about **$1,000** of stock. The target is about
  5% up (about +$50). The stop is about 3% down (about −$30).
- **Simulated vs paper:**
  - *Simulated* is what the rule says should have happened, graded from
    price bars.
  - *Paper* is what actually filled in the Alpaca paper account.
  - The records give dollar amounts only for the paper fills. On every
    counted trade this week, the simulated result (win, loss or time exit)
    matched the paper result. The one mismatch is IVA on 9/30. So the
    simulated dollar result is about the same as the paper result: the 10/2
    evening report says slippage was "a few cents". There is no separate
    simulated dollar total in the records.
- **Two versions of one strategy:** "intraday continuation" runs as v1 and v2
  side by side. They share **one** paper order, so each trade below is
  counted once.
- **Data feed:** all week, Ghost ran on the free IEX quote feed. The paid
  real-time feed (SIP, the full-market feed) answers "403 not authorized".
  That makes every strategy "degraded". Each morning only 2 to 6 of 25 to 28
  card candidates had a fresh premarket price.
